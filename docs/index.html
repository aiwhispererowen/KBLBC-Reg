Tournament registration: board preview

A working mock-up of the proposed replacement for SignUpGenius. Try it the way a player or a captain would.

How it fits together

  1. A tournament on the calendar is a link to its registration form (the gold entries).
  2. Each tournament on the Tournaments and In Club Tournaments pages has a Register button to the same form.
  3. The captain fills in the form and gets a confirmation email; the organizers get a copy.

Things to try

  • Register for Aussie Pairs: there is no third-player field, and the second player is optional.
  • Register for Canada vs Rest of the World: the Canada / ROW choice is required.
  • Use the Demo mode box at the bottom of a form to fill the tournament and see the waitlist, or to close registration.
  • Open the cancel link from a confirmation email (shown in the demo box).
